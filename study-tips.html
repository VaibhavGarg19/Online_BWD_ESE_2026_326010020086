Build helpful study habits

Try one idea at a time and see what works best for you.

1

Choose one goal

Before you begin, decide on one small task, such as reading two pages or answering five questions.

2

Find a quiet place

Keep the things you need nearby and put away anything that might distract you.

3

Take short breaks

After a focused study session, stand up, stretch, and give your mind a little rest.

4

Explain what you learned

Try to describe a new idea in your own words. This can help you notice what to review next.

5

Review a little often

Looking back at your notes regularly can be easier than trying to learn everything at once.

6

Celebrate your effort

Finishing a planned task is progress. Give yourself credit and make a fresh plan for tomorrow.

Make a plan that works for you, one day at a time.
